Abhaya Hridaya Mudra

the Fearless Heart Mudra · courage from the heart

The fearless heart. An interlaced gesture held at the heart centre, Abhaya Hridaya Mudra dissolves fear and awakens courage — a reminder of the deep well of peace and strength you carry within.

  • MeaningFearless heart
  • CentreThe heart
  • HandsBacks together, fingers interlaced
  • FingersRing finger to thumb each hand
  • Hold for10 – 15 minutes
  • Best forCourage, calm, inner strength

What Abhaya Hridaya Mudra means

Abhaya Hridaya Mudra is a gesture of the fearless heart. It works to eliminate fear and awaken courage, standing as a reminder of your own inner strength and drawing out the essence of your true nature.

Where the simpler Abhaya Mudra offers protection with a single raised palm, this is its deeper, heart-centred cousin — a bound, interlaced gesture held at the chest. If you long to connect with the deep well of peace, strength and courage that lives within your heart, this is a beautiful practice to explore.

❤

Courage lives in the heart

The gesture gathers both hands together at the heart, fingers woven into one. It is a physical picture of turning inward to the heart's own steadiness — the place where real courage is found.

How to practise Abhaya Hridaya Mudra

  1. Sit and settleFind a comfortable seated position and take a few slow breaths to relax.
  2. Cross the wrists at the heartCross your right wrist in front of your left at the centre of your chest, and bring the backs of the hands together.
  3. Interlock the fingersHook together the opposite little fingers, ring fingers and index fingers — skipping the middle fingers, which stay free.
  4. Join ring finger and thumbOn each hand, bring the tip of the ring finger to meet the tip of the thumb.
  5. Relax and breatheLet the gesture settle, breathe naturally, and rest here for 10 to 15 minutes.

Tip: this is an intricate bind — take it slowly the first few times. Work the fingers into place gently rather than forcing them, and ease off if your hands feel strained.

Because the bind takes a little setting up, give yourself a quiet, unhurried space to settle into it.

Practising with care

  • The interlocking of the fingers can feel awkward at first — build it up gently and never force the hands into place.
  • Release if you feel any strain in the fingers, wrists or shoulders, and keep the breath easy throughout.
  • Mudras complement a healthy lifestyle and a calm mind — they are a support for courage and wellbeing, not a treatment. If fear or anxiety weighs heavily on you, be gentle with yourself and reach out for support.

Abhaya Hridaya Mudra — common questions

What is Abhaya Hridaya Mudra used for?
It is the gesture of the fearless heart — used to dissolve fear, awaken courage and inner strength, calm scattered energy, and connect you with the peace and strength within your heart.
How do you form Abhaya Hridaya Mudra?
Cross the right wrist in front of the left at the heart, backs of the hands together. Interlock the opposite little, ring and index fingers (skipping the middle fingers), then bring the ring finger and thumb together on each hand.
How is it different from Abhaya Mudra?
Abhaya Mudra is a simple raised-palm gesture of protection. Abhaya Hridaya Mudra is a more intricate, interlaced bind held at the heart, focused on awakening courage from within.
How long should I hold Abhaya Hridaya Mudra?
Around 10 to 15 minutes, breathing naturally.
Why are the middle fingers left out?
In this bind you interlock the little, ring and index fingers while the middle fingers stay free — this is simply the specific finger arrangement that forms the gesture.
